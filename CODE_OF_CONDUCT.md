# Code of conduct

Quanty is developed by scientists, students and users from many countries and backgrounds. We want everybody who works on Quanty, reports a bug or asks a question on the forum to feel welcome.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and friendly, also when you disagree.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Help newcomers. Everybody once asked their first question.
- Give credit: name the people whose code, ideas or results you use, and cite their work.
- Respect the time of the maintainers. Quanty is maintained by a small group next to their research and teaching.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults or discriminating remarks of any kind.
- Personal attacks or deliberately disrupting discussions.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

If someone behaves in a way that is not acceptable, please let Maurits know by [e-mail](mailto:m.w.haverkort@thphys.uni-heidelberg.de). Reports are treated confidentially. Maurits may warn, or exclude from the project, anybody who does not follow this code of conduct.

This code of conduct applies to the repositories, the forum at [www.quanty.org](https://www.quanty.org), and to meetings and workshops about Quanty.
